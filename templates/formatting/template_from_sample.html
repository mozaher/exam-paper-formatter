How it works

  1. Cleaning: Word files are unconditionally stripped of macros, DDE fields and embedded objects — by category, no exceptions.
  2. Locating the questions: the dummy-question area is detected automatically (numbered questions, A)/B)/C) options, marks). If it can't be found confidently, you'll simply point out where the questions start and end.
  3. Preview & confirm: you see your own document with sample questions injected. One click saves it as a template.
  4. Generation: every paper replaces only the questions area (and recognized fill-in fields like Subject/Date/Time). Everything else in your file is preserved byte-for-byte. LaTeX templates are compiled in a locked-down sandbox — no shell access, no network, strict limits.

Tips: include at least two dummy questions (with A)/B)/C) options for multiple choice), and leave the blank space or answer boxes you want after essay questions. In LaTeX you can guarantee detection by wrapping the block in %%QUESTIONS_START … %%QUESTIONS_END comments.
