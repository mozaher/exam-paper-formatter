How it works

  1. Your file is rendered in a locked-down sandbox (no network, strict time and memory limits) and measured: fonts, margins, line and paragraph spacing.
  2. Detected regions are labelled — question text, marks like “[5 marks]”, and the blank answer space beneath them — to learn how much space you leave per mark.
  3. If everything is clear, your template is created immediately. If anything was ambiguous, you'll see a side-by-side comparison and simple controls to fix it — no technical settings involved.

Tips for best results: include 2–3 questions with different marks (e.g. [2 marks] and [5 marks]) followed by the blank space you'd leave for answers, and at least one paragraph long enough to wrap over several lines.
Privacy: the uploaded file is analysed and immediately discarded — only the confirmed formatting settings are stored.
